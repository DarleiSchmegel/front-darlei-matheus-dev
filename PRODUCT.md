# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 12, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 3, next-themes, phosphor-react. Existing codebase is the stack authority; no user decision required.

## Users

- Tech recruiters and hiring managers (BR and international) evaluating Darlei for senior full-stack roles
- Tech leads and CTOs assessing engineering depth and architectural judgment
- Potential SaaS clients and partners who discover him through products he builds
- Peers in engineering who evaluate the work through technical writing and case studies

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and professional identity site. Presents Darlei M. Schmegel — Software Engineer · Product Builder — through real work evidence rather than claims: professional trajectory, selected projects, engineering practices, and a direct contact channel. Success means a visitor leaves convinced of his competence as a full-stack engineer with product vision and reaches out or explores deeper.

## Positioning

Not a developer who lists technologies — an engineer who combines deep technical practice (enterprise ERP at Softplan/Sienge, high-volume trading platforms at Nelogica), product vision (own SaaS products for real businesses), and process engineering (AI-assisted spec-driven development workflows). The site must show what he builds and how he thinks, not what adjectives he claims.

## Operating Context

- First impression happens in under 30 seconds: the homepage must carry positioning, proof, and a next step without scrolling twice
- Deeper evaluation happens on dedicated pages: experience detail, project case studies, full biography
- Shared via LinkedIn, GitHub profile, and CV header links; visitors arrive mid-funnel already knowing the name

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual readiness: primary content in Portuguese (BR audience), positioning labels in English for international signal
- No backend: static/SSG site; contact page provides direct channels (email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn) rather than a server-side form
- Photo asset available: `public/assets/darlei.jpg` (real photo from CV)
- Copy must stay true to the CV facts in `CV_Darlei/Darlei.tex` and the positioning dossier in `docs/sobre-mim-gpt.md` — no invented metrics, clients, or outcomes

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: analytical, direct, confident without self-promotion inflation — Portuguese body copy, English for short positioning labels
- Name format: "Darlei M. Schmegel" / "Darlei Matheus Schmegel"; domain identity `darlei.dev`
- Tagline: "Software Engineer · Product Builder"
- Personality axes (from sobre-mim-gpt.md): racional e analítico, técnico e criterioso, construtor e realizador, contemporâneo e conectado, discreto mas confiante

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/sobre-mim-gpt.md` — positioning dossier: four-dimension profile (engineering, architecture/quality, product building, process engineering), recommended palette and typography, sample about-me copy
- `CV_Darlei/Darlei.tex` — factual career record: UFPel Computer Engineering (2018–2022), Sienge ERP at Starian/Softplan (2025–present), Nelogica trading platforms (2023–2025), App Masters frontend (2021–2022), PET Computação (2019–2023), freelance SaaS since 2021, 1st place UFPel Hackathon
- `public/assets/darlei.jpg` — real photo
- No real project screenshots beyond `nft_l2.png`; portfolio sections must lean on case-study copy, not fabricated imagery

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives — show projects, decisions, and outcomes; never list claims
2. Editorial engineering — precise hierarchy, technical details, and generous whitespace like a well-built developer tool
3. Product truth — every fact on the page must trace to the CV or the dossier
4. Quiet confidence — modern and technical without neon-terminal clichés
